03 / About this tool

DNAInsight is a student-built educational project for learning sequence analysis. GC content is the percentage of bases that are guanine or cytosine. Transcription here uses the coding-strand convention: replace T with U.

For real research, record the source and accession of each sequence, explain your methods, validate results independently and cite relevant literature.
